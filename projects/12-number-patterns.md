# Number Patterns

## Exercise

Print the following five patterns using nested loops:

```text
*****       12345       *****       12345       1
****        1234        ****        1234        12
***         123         ***         123         123
**          12          **          12          1234
*           1           *           1           12345
```

## Concepts practiced

Nested loops, row/column thinking, and formatted output.
