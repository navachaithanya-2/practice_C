# Operators and Macros

## Exercises

1. Use a macro to calculate the area and circumference of a circle.
2. Use macros to calculate the square and cube of a number.
3. Check whether a number is even or odd using the remainder operator.
4. Swap two numbers without a temporary variable.

## Concepts practiced

`#define`, arithmetic operators, assignment, and operator precedence.
